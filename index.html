Glance

A calm, always-on iPad dashboard for students: clocks, homework, calendar, tasks, weather and a live flight radar, all on one screen.

  • Homework from PRONOTE, shown exactly as teachers wrote it.
  • Calendar and tasks from Google (optional) and the iPad itself.
  • Weather for any city, and a flight radar of planes overhead.
  • No ads, no tracking, and data stays on the iPad.

Glance is a small private project for a group of students, not a commercial product.
